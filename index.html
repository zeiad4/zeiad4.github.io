I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at Iowa State University, working with Prof. Ossama Abdelkhalik in the Department of Aerospace Engineering. My current research explores diffusion-model-based control of wave energy converters under irregular ocean wave conditions.

Previously, I worked on reinforcement learning for wave energy control at Iowa State and deep learning for coronary artery segmentation at Istanbul Technical University. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from Istanbul Bilgi University in 2026.

Research interests: Generative AI, control theory, and robotics.

Current research

Generative AI for wave energy converter control

Research Assistant · Iowa State University · Aug. 2026–present

I develop and evaluate diffusion-model-based control approaches for wave energy systems in stochastic, irregular seas. This work combines machine learning, numerical simulation, and control methods to investigate strategies for improving energy capture and system performance.
